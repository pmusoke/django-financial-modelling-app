This financial modelling application is inspired by the Gridlines solar project finance model. It allows users to evaluate the financial performance of a solar energy project using configurable operating and financing assumptions.

Overall Assumptions

This application models the financial performance of a solar energy project to evaluate its investment viability. It generates quarterly financial statements using assumptions relating to electricity generation, operating expenses, financing and taxation.

Electricity Generation

That with the electricity generation, that there is a maximum availability of GWh per year

That the solar plant will operate at a percentage of power production and the generation will be subjected to a percentage perfromance degradation per annum

That 100% of the annual prodcution will be split up into 4 quarters

That theres an unindexed power tarrif at cents/KWh

That electiricty generation is subjected to a percentage performance degredation per annum

Expense Assumptions

Operating and maintenance expenses are based on an agreed fixed annual fee.

Indexation is applied annually on the anniversary of the beginning of the project

Debt Assumptions

Interest and principal on the senior loan will be paid quarterly

Revolving Credit Facility

The facility ranks below senior but above any shareholder distributions in the cash flow waterfall
